## Three critical engineering questions

**Why do hashes matter if the same operator owns the host?** They bind the exact material revision and executable payload within this application. The executor independently rechecks that binding and current constraints. They expose stale or mutated proposals under the tested application model; they are not operator-resistant attestation, signatures or proof that telemetry is true.

**What does Evaluate contribute beyond the local if-statements?** It provides the actual bounded selection/refusal and trace for a finite verification activity. The app maps that result to its exact local proposal, records the real envelope, performs a fresh local check and retains human authority. The local rules, not a score, enforce capacity and freshness. No unmeasured claim says the selector itself improved recovery metrics.

**What happens if a response, process or write is uncertain?** The request body/key are reserved before dispatch. Unknown outcomes remain counted and lock that identity; no automatic retry or fresh-key escape occurs. A receipt that cannot be durably recorded yields no usable selection. Worker delivery failures are consumed/unknown; lost in-flight jobs are not silently replayed. Child termination must be observed before the rig lease is released. Missing or invalid request history disables hosted access.
